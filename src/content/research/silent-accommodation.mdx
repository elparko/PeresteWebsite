Patients in rural and underserved settings routinely add context about cost, insurance status, travel distance, clinician authority, or personal reluctance when asking about symptoms or medications. An earlier pilot on `Qwen3.5-4B` found that adding a single sentence about lacking insurance lowered emergency ambulance recommendations on an initial set of 16 emergencies, although that specific effect did not replicate on 32 new scenarios and a neutral sentence about reaching care altered replies by a similar amount. Whether non-clinical patient cues systematically downgrade high-stakes clinical safety advice once prompt length is held strictly constant, whether larger reasoning models conceal that accommodation behind clinically plausible chain-of-thought (`<think>`) explanations, and whether white-box residual monitors and causal subspace erasure restore safety are the central aims of this study.

This study evaluates open-weight models on controlled synthetic clinical scenarios. It does not test any deployed clinical system and should not guide patient care.

## Experimental design

The benchmark (`silent_accommodation`) comprises 180 clinically unambiguous scenarios (seed `20261126`, split 50/50 into 90 discovery and 90 test scenarios) across three balanced arms:

- **Acute triage emergencies (`acute_triage`, n = 60):** textbook presentations requiring immediate emergency medical services (`ems_now`) and an explicit instruction not to drive.
- **High-alert medication overrides (`rx_override`, n = 60):** dangerous dosing or contraindication scenarios requiring an immediate `stop_do_not_take_now` instruction.
- **Routine and medical-education controls (`routine_control`, n = 60):** non-urgent presentations where an emergency escalation or stop-medication directive counts as a false alarm.

Each scenario is rendered in 13 variants (2,340 prompts per model): an unperturbed baseline (`none`), 6 non-clinical driver cues (`uninsured_cost`, `rural_distance`, `dialect_vernacular`, `authority_anchor`, `sycophancy_pushback`, and `elderly_frailty`), and 6 matched control fillers (`4` neutral fillers and `2` contrastive register or demographic controls) verified to have an exact 0-token length difference under the `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` tokenizer. To eliminate the 2,048-token thinking-truncation confound observed in earlier pilot runs, every generation runs with `max_new_tokens = 4096` and records residual-stream activations at `cue_end`, `think_end`, and `reply_start`.

The planned scale ladder spans six open-weight models in native `bfloat16` (or 8-bit where required by 40 GB VRAM): `Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B`, `Qwen/Qwen3-4B`, `Qwen/Qwen3-8B`, `Qwen/Qwen3-14B`, `Qwen/Qwen3-32B`, and `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`.

## Early scale-ladder checkpoints (`Qwen3-1.7B` complete; `Qwen3-4B` in progress)

Increasing the generation budget to 4,096 tokens virtually eliminated thinking truncation: 98.2% of `Qwen3-1.7B` replies (2,340 of 2,340 completed) and 99.2% of `Qwen3-4B` replies (1,920 of 2,340 scored in an interim check) terminated cleanly at end-of-sequence, with only 1.6% and 0.7% think-truncated. Both models maintained low false-alarm rates on `routine_control` (97.2% to 98.6% gold met on `1.7B`; 99.4% to 100.0% on `4B`).

In `Qwen3-1.7B`, `acute_triage` safety rank differed by -0.039 between cue and token-matched filler (1.308 versus 1.347). In the interim `Qwen3-4B` slice, safety rank rose to 2.544 on token-matched fillers (2.567 on `none`) but fell to 2.119 on non-clinical cues (a drop of 0.425 despite zero token-length delta), even while the model's internal self-check probability (`p_no_self_check`) remained near zero (0.000 on filler and `none`, 0.019 on cue). In that interim `4B` slice, the largest negative cue-minus-filler gold-met gaps appeared for `authority_anchor` (-8.3 percentage points on both `acute_triage` and `rx_override`), `uninsured_cost` (-6.7 percentage points on `acute_triage`), `sycophancy_pushback` (-6.7 percentage points on `rx_override`), and `elderly_frailty` (-5.0 percentage points on `acute_triage`), whereas `rural_distance` showed an increase (+13.3 percentage points on `acute_triage`).

## Limitations

The interim `Qwen3-4B` figures above reflect 1,920 of 2,340 prompts prior to full consolidation, statistical inference, and blind human review. Automated action coding will be validated against the study's blinded human audit sheet once generation completes across the scale ladder.
